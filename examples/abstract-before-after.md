# Example — Abstract Audit Pattern

## Before

The material is robust, scalable and sustainable, and shows excellent performance in many applications.

## Problem

Decorative triple; no metric; no field calibration; overbroad application claim.

## After

The material was prepared on the gram scale, retained its target property after processing and produced a controlled proof-of-concept response in [setting].

## Why better

Specific, bounded, defensible.
