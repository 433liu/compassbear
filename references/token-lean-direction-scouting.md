# Token-Lean Direction Scouting

Use this before full-paper reading when the user asks whether a direction is promising, crowded, weak or worth deeper investment.

## T0/T1 Scout

Return:

- direction map;
- 3-5 sentinel source or search leads;
- one adversarial route;
- crowding signal;
- strongest possible framing;
- reason to stop or escalate;
- next cheapest action.

Do not claim novelty from exact-keyword absence. Escalate to full reading when the next decision depends on citable evidence or detailed mechanism comparison.
