# Iteration Discipline

Quality plateaus after 3–5 substantive revisions of a given section. After that, further edits often over-formalize the voice, dilute the claim or reintroduce old problems.

## Stop signals

Stop revising the same section when:

- the latest revision changes only 5–10% of the previous draft;
- the last two edits are aesthetic rather than substantive;
- no new inconsistency is found;
- the text is within the target word range;
- the user has accepted the section twice;
- the remaining problems are in downstream documents.

## Locking output

When stopping, state:

- what is locked;
- what remains open;
- what downstream documents need propagation;
- what the next bottleneck is.

Do not restart revision unless the user explicitly asks.
