# Journal Style Profiles

Use this reference when the user asks to adapt a manuscript, abstract, title,
cover letter, figure logic or claim hierarchy for a target journal or journal
family.

This is not phrase mimicry. It is journal-positioning conversion: audience,
claim level, evidence emphasis, article type, figure density and boundary
language should change while the scientific meaning remains stable.

## Shared conversion workflow

1. Identify target journal and article type.
2. Preserve the evidence boundary before changing style.
3. Decide the journal's reader promise:
   - concept for broad chemistry;
   - urgent chemical result;
   - functional material advance;
   - method/platform;
   - application-facing performance.
4. Rebuild title, abstract opening, figure spine and cover-letter hook around
   that promise.
5. Demote any claim that the target journal would likely see as routine,
   applied-only, over-specialized, or unsupported.

## JACS profile

**Positioning.** JACS is a broad chemistry journal for fundamental research in
all fields of chemistry. It prioritizes high scientific quality, originality,
significance, conceptual novelty and interest to a wide chemistry readership.

**Best fit when:**

- the work changes how chemists understand a reaction, mechanism, structure,
  bonding, design rule, synthesis logic or chemical principle;
- the advance is not only a device/application improvement;
- methodology papers include broadly interesting applications or conceptual
  advance;
- the work can be read by chemists outside the narrow subfield.

**Weak fit when:**

- the claim is mainly applied performance without chemical insight;
- the work is a routine extension or scope expansion;
- a previous Communication exists and the new paper adds data without a new
  concept;
- the story depends on engineering utility more than chemical principle.

**Style conversion.**

- Lead with the chemical problem and conceptual advance.
- Explain why the result matters to chemists outside the immediate field.
- Keep literature review tight.
- Make the opening sentences carry the principal conclusion for Communication
  style.
- Use mechanism and scope honestly; JACS readers will punish decorative breadth.

**Article-type cues.**

- Communication: urgency, timeliness, significance, broad interest, concise
  scientific findings.
- Article: complete story with proper sections and durable experimental or
  theoretical value.
- Perspective: focused personal review that assesses current status and future
  directions.

## Angew profile

**Positioning.** Angewandte Chemie is a GDCh/Wiley-VCH general chemistry journal
with a weekly mix of review-type articles, Highlights, Communications and
Research Articles. It is designed for chemists who want to stay informed across
major and interdisciplinary areas of chemistry.

**Best fit when:**

- the work is chemically surprising, timely and compact;
- the result is broad enough for general chemists, not only a specialist
  materials or methods audience;
- the main claim can be communicated as a sharp chemical advance;
- the manuscript has a strong figure/scheme spine and immediate novelty signal.

**Weak fit when:**

- the story needs extensive application validation to be convincing;
- the key advance is gradual optimization rather than a new chemical insight;
- the evidence is too bulky or incremental for a communication-style argument;
- review/minireview/highlight content is unsolicited and not editor-approved.

**Style conversion.**

- Lead with the surprising chemical result or conceptual problem.
- Use a compact, high-signal opening.
- Prefer a clear scheme/figure that reveals the chemical advance quickly.
- Keep background short and problem-driven.
- Avoid turning a Communication into a compressed full paper by hiding load
  bearing evidence in the SI.

**Article-type cues.**

- Communications and Research Articles report latest research results selected
  critically.
- Review-type articles summarize recent topical research, unresolved problems
  and possible developments; proposals should be assessed by the editorial office.

## Advanced Materials profile

**Positioning.** Advanced Materials publishes top-quality Research Articles,
Reviews and Perspectives at the cutting edge of the chemistry and physics of
functional materials. Its stated strength is pronounced interdisciplinarity.

**Best fit when:**

- the central object is a functional material, material platform, device-relevant
  property or material-enabled application;
- performance, function, mechanism and material design are all visible;
- the result speaks to materials scientists, chemists, physicists and engineers;
- the figures can show a design-function-performance path.

**Weak fit when:**

- the work is mainly a chemical mechanism without material function;
- performance improvement lacks a material design principle;
- application claims exceed validation conditions;
- the result is only one optimized sample without broader material insight.

**Style conversion.**

- Lead with the material function and why it matters.
- Make the design principle and performance consequence visible early.
- Tie mechanism to material behavior rather than leaving it as isolated
  spectroscopy.
- Use figures to connect structure, property, function and application.
- Keep application language bounded by tested conditions.

**Article-type cues.**

- Research Article: fuller materials story with design, characterization,
  mechanism/function and performance.
- Review/Perspective: field-level synthesis and forward-looking analysis.

## Fast target selection

| Project center | Better initial target |
|---|---|
| new chemical principle / mechanism / reaction logic | JACS or Angew |
| compact, urgent, chemically surprising result | Angew |
| broad fundamental chemistry with durable conceptual advance | JACS |
| functional material platform with performance and application signal | Advanced Materials |
| device/application story without strong chemical/material principle | likely a specialist applied journal |
| method/tool without broad validation or benchmark | specialist methods journal unless upgraded |

## Style-transfer examples

Same scientific core, different framing:

| Target | Opening move |
|---|---|
| JACS | "A central question in [chemistry area] is whether [chemical principle] can be controlled through [design variable]." |
| Angew | "Here we report a [surprising chemical result] that reveals [principle] in [system]." |
| Advanced Materials | "Functional materials that [application/function] require [design challenge]; here, [material strategy] enables [measured function] under [scope]." |

## Guardrails

- Do not change the evidence level to fit a journal.
- Do not use journal prestige to justify overclaiming.
- Do not make Advanced Materials framing purely application hype.
- Do not make JACS framing purely "fundamental" if the chemical insight is thin.
- Do not make Angew framing urgent unless the result is genuinely compact,
  timely and broadly interesting.
