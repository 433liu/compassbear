# Methods Structure

## Strong Methods modules

- General information
- Materials
- Synthesis / sample preparation
- Characterization
- Computation / modelling
- Functional testing
- Statistics and reproducibility
- Data/code availability

## Rules

- Methods should be reproducible, not narrative-heavy.
- Put detailed parameter sweeps and full tables in SI.
- Do not hide essential experimental conditions in captions.
- Avoid duplicated method paragraphs.
