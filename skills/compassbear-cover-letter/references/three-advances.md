# Three Advances

## Purpose

The letter should make the paper feel like more than a one-result story.

## Recommended categories

- New material/platform/system
- New mechanism/design rule
- New function/validation/deployment context

## Balance rule

If the paper uses AI, computation or automation, do not let those tools occupy more than one of the three main advances unless the paper is truly a methods paper.
