# Material vs Application Patents

## Material-platform patent

Protects:

- composition;
- synthesis route;
- structural variables;
- property tuning;
- broad non-exclusive applications.

## Application-product patent

Protects:

- product composition;
- device/film/formulation;
- loading/thickness/process;
- use scenario;
- performance function.

## Split rule

Do not let the application patent's independent claim simply repeat the material patent's synthesis method. Anchor it in the product or use.
