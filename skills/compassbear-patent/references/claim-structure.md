# Patent Claim Structure

## Common independent claim types

- Composition of matter
- Preparation method
- Device/product
- Use/application
- System/workflow

## Claim drafting principle

The independent claim protects the broad inventive concept; dependent claims protect preferred embodiments, ranges and applications.

## Avoid

- making the independent claim too narrow;
- mixing unrelated inventions in one claim;
- claiming data rather than technical features;
- overlapping two related patent filings completely.
