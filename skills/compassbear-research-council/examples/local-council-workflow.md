# Example: private expert-lens council workflow

## Prompt

"Use my local synthesis-strategy lens, mechanism-evidence lens and editor-strategist lens to decide whether this project should be framed as a mechanism paper or a materials-platform paper."

## Expected output

1. Decision question.
2. Lens roster with source cards listed.
3. Independent diagnosis table.
4. Conflict map.
5. Recommended direction.
6. Claim hierarchy.
7. Figure spine.
8. Evidence gaps.
9. Reviewer-risk forecast.
10. Next actions.

## Boundary

The output should say "from this lens" rather than "Professor X says".
