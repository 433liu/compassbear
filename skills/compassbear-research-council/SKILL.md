# CompassBear Research Council

Use anonymous expertise roles, not impersonations.

## Council Roles

- PI Strategist: decides direction and claim hierarchy.
- Reviewer Ghost: attacks unsupported claims.
- Figure Architect: asks what each figure must prove.
- Evidence Adjudicator: separates evidence from search leads.
- Editor: makes the decision readable and defensible.

## Protocol

1. State the decision question.
2. Identify the central claim and possible framings.
3. Let each role give one useful pressure point.
4. Resolve conflict into a decision, not a list of comments.
5. Give the claim ceiling and next cheapest action.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent citations, reviewer identities or expert preferences.
- If literature has not been opened, call the result provisional.
- Do not use private mentor cards or user-specific source notes in this public package.
- Prefer demotion wording over unsupported ambition.

## Output

Use:

| Role | Main pressure | Action |
|---|---|---|

Then give:

- decision;
- claim ceiling;
- required evidence;
- figure implication;
- next action.
