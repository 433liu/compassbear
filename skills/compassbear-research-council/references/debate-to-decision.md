# Debate-to-decision synthesis

A useful council session ends with a decision. Use this output structure.

## Decision matrix

| Candidate direction | Strength | Weakness | Evidence needed | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Recommendation labels:

- MAIN: make it the paper's spine
- SUPPORT: keep as supporting angle
- SI: move to supplement
- TEST: needs evidence before claiming
- DROP: remove or postpone

## Claim hierarchy output

1. Central claim
2. Section claims
3. Figure claims
4. Claims to demote
5. Claims to avoid

## Action list

Separate actions by time horizon:

- next 24 hours: wording, figure map, evidence table
- next 7 days: analysis, control, SI cleanup
- next 30 days: experiment, validation, collaboration
