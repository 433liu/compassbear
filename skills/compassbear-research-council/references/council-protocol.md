# Council Protocol

Use the council when a research decision needs structured pressure rather than prose polish.

## Steps

1. Define the decision question.
2. List the candidate framings.
3. Ask each role for one objection or requirement.
4. Identify the evidence owner for the central claim.
5. Decide whether to proceed, demote, search more or stop.

## Anti-Patterns

- many generic comments with no decision;
- named-expert imitation;
- novelty claims from shallow search;
- figure suggestions not tied to claims;
- polishing language before fixing evidence.

## Output Shape

Give a short memo, then a table:

| Framing | Upside | Risk | Evidence needed | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
