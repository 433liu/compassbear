# Term Audit

## Terms to align

- sample names;
- material names;
- mechanism names;
- model names;
- measurement regions;
- biological endpoints;
- deployment scenarios;
- statistical metrics.

## Risky synonym drift

- yield vs biomass vs fresh weight;
- validation vs prediction vs scenario;
- condition A vs condition B vs condition C;
- intrinsic behavior vs treatment-derived behavior;
- scalable vs scale-demonstrated;
- biodegradable vs degradation observed under specific conditions.
