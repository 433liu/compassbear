# Human Scientific Voice

## Goal

Reduce template rhythm while preserving scientific precision.

## Common AI markers

- evenly weighted long sentences;
- repeated `X enables Y` structures;
- three-item lists in consecutive paragraphs;
- `Together, these results establish...` used repeatedly;
- generic adjectives: robust, scalable, sustainable, versatile;
- method phrases that sound impressive but do not change the claim.

## Humanization moves

- Break one overloaded sentence into two sentences with unequal weight.
- Replace decorative triples with the one property that matters.
- Use concrete nouns instead of abstract praise.
- Let important numbers stand alone.
- Vary sentence rhythm without making the prose casual.
- Prefer direct claims over meta-commentary.

## Do not

- Add slang.
- Remove necessary technical terms.
- Weaken a supported claim just to sound modest.
- Change scientific meaning while polishing voice.
