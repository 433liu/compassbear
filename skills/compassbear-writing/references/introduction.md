# Introduction

## Goal

Move from field-scale need to precise unresolved gap, then to the present study.

## Architecture

1. Field-scale need
2. Existing approach and why it matters
3. Material/conceptual barrier
4. Opportunity created by this work
5. Present study and key advances

## Avoid

- Review-style encyclopedic opening;
- over-listing weaknesses of prior work;
- declaring a gap that the paper does not actually close;
- method-first framing before the material/science problem is clear.

## Good final paragraph

The final Introduction paragraph should connect:

- what was built or discovered;
- why the approach was needed;
- what rule/mechanism/function emerged;
- what boundary is respected.
