# Conclusion

## Purpose

The conclusion should not simply repeat the abstract. It should state what the work changes, what can be reused, and where the boundary lies.

## Recommended structure

1. Main material/conceptual contribution
2. Transferable rule or workflow
3. Functional validation
4. Boundary and next step
5. Final platform implication

## Boundary sentence patterns

- `These results should be viewed as [validation level], rather than [unvalidated claim].`
- `The broader conclusion is that...`
- `This work provides a route to..., while leaving... for future validation.`

## Avoid

- `This opens a new era...`
- `This will revolutionize...`
- unsupported commercial or clinical/agricultural deployment claims.
