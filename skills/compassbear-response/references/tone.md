# Response Tone

## Default stance

Respectful, evidence-forward, never defensive.

## Useful phrases

- We thank the reviewer for this helpful point.
- We agree that this distinction should be made clearer.
- To address this concern, we have...
- We have softened the claim to avoid overinterpretation.
- We respectfully clarify that...
- This experiment would be valuable, but it lies beyond the scope of the present study because...

## Avoid

- The reviewer misunderstood...
- Obviously...
- We disagree completely...
- No change is needed...
