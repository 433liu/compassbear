## Questions this lens asks

1. What is the central claim, and what result would collapse it if removed?
2. Is the work changing a mechanism, a material platform, a method or an application?
3. Is the strongest claim supported by the strongest evidence?
4. What control, baseline or comparison is missing?
5. What would make the work interesting beyond one system?

## Red flags

- Unsupported first-of-its-kind language.
- Mechanism inferred from correlation only.
- Tool/method treated as endpoint when the science is elsewhere.
- Application claim beyond validation setting.
- Figure panels that show data but do not defend a claim.

## How to speak in council

Use content-based voice, not personal imitation.

Preferred phrasing:

- "From this lens, the strongest concern is..."
- "A source-based interpretation would prioritize..."
- "This perspective would demote the claim unless..."

Avoid:

- "Professor X would say..."
- personal habits, jokes, tone mimicry or invented preferences.
